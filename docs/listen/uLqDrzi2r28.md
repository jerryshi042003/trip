## Key points
- [0:00:32] Adam is in Cebu City at the Asmara Urban Resort giving lessons to local kids; with beginners he alternates swing work and footwork, because a good swing collapses once players start moving.
- [0:01:23] The four steps in almost every shot except the serve: split step, turn (racket back early), move, then adjust.
- [0:02:09] Turning and getting the racket back first can save a shot even when the feet are late; a late racket means the ball will not go in.
- [0:02:37] Drills need a purpose and a progression: hand feeding at a slow pace, then feeding from the other side of the net, then variations.
- [0:03:01] He brought rackets, bags and shirts to donate and told the director the gear must go to the kids, since equipment is expensive in the Philippines and can change a kid's life.
- [0:04:37] Footwork vocabulary for kids: the crossover step is "taking a car" for long distances, the side step is a shorter "bike," then small adjustment steps.
